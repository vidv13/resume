1-year reserved instances

Roughly 40% off node pool VMs. Compute is the largest platform line, so this is the highest-leverage move. Commit after one or two months of stable usage, not on day one.

Scale non-prod overnight

—

Dev and staging run 10 hours a day, 5 days a week instead of 24/7. Azure Automation or a scheduled scale-down job. Toggle it above to see the effect.

Firewall SKU right-sizing

~$366/mo

Dropping the hub firewall from Premium to Standard saves roughly this much — but Premium's IDPS is what MLZ relies on for the SCCA VDSS control. This is a compliance decision, not a cost decision. Raise it with whoever owns the accreditation.

How to read the two totals: the MLZ shared line is what the landing zone costs to exist, and in most organisations running MLZ it's already funded by whoever operates the hub. If your team is being charged back for it, add it. If the hub already exists for other workloads, your platform's real incremental cost is the platform total only.

Not included:
  • Databases — Azure SQL, PostgreSQL Flexible, or Cosmos DB, budgeted per app. Figure $50–200/mo each depending on tier, plus a private endpoint apiece.
  • Azure DevOps user licences — free to 5 users, then $6/user/mo.
  • Microsoft Defender for Containers — worth pricing separately; it's commonly required alongside an SCCA posture.
  • Backup, DR, and any Azure Policy remediation tasks MLZ triggers.
  • The engineering time to stand this up: realistically 4–8 weeks for the first cluster in an MLZ context, versus 2–4 weeks greenfield. The private networking is where the time goes.

Verify before you budget: these are US-region list prices assembled from public pricing pages and are directional. Rates move, and any Enterprise Agreement or MACC discount you have will change the picture materially. Run the final numbers through the Azure Pricing Calculator with your actual region and agreement before taking them to a finance conversation.
